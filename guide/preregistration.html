Pre-registered analysis plan#

The plan below was committed to version control before any epoch, feature or model output existed. Its git history (git log -- docs/preregistration.md) proves the order. Every later change is listed in Deviations.

Analysis plan, fixed before preprocessing and modelling#

Date fixed: 2026-11-04. This file is committed to git before any epochs, features or model results exist (see git log -- docs/preregistration.md). Any later deviation is listed in reports/deviations.md with its reason.

Data#

  • MODMA, 128-channel HydroCel GSN (EGI) ERP dot-probe task, Lanzhou 2015 recordings.

  • 53 recordings, one per participant: 24 MDD, 29 HC. Labels come only from subjects_information_EEG_128channels_ERP_lanzhou_2015.xlsx, column type.

  • The three files marked “new” or “(n)” (02010008, 02020023, 02030002) are the only recording for their participant and contain the full task. They are included.

  • Task: fixation → face pair (emotional + neutral, 516 ms) → ISI (116–316 ms) → dot probe. Three blocks of 160 trials (fear, happy, sad).

Preprocessing (per participant; nothing is fitted across participants)#

  1. Read the .raw file. Rename E129 (the recording reference) to Cz and set the GSN-HydroCel-129 montage.

  2. Events are read by EGI channel name with fixed codes: fcue→11 (fear), hcue→12 (happy), scue→13 (sad). Epochs are locked to face-pair onset.

  3. Bad channels are detected automatically on a 1–40 Hz copy. A channel is bad if it meets any of these:

    • It is flat (SD < 0.1 µV).

    • Its robust z-score of log-SD is above 5 in absolute value.

    • Its median correlation with its 6 nearest neighbours is below 0.4.

    E129/Cz is the recording reference, so it is exempt from the flat check.

  4. ICA is fitted on a copy filtered 1–100 Hz, notched at 50 Hz and average-referenced, with bad channels excluded and the data decimated by 2. It uses Picard (extended infomax), 30 components and seed 97. Components are labelled with ICLabel. A component is removed when its top label is eye blink, muscle artifact, heart beat, line noise or channel noise and that label’s probability is at least 0.80. Decisions are written to configs/ica_exclusions.csv.

  5. Analysis copy: 0.5–40 Hz zero-phase FIR band-pass, 50 Hz notch, ICA applied, bad channels interpolated (spherical spline), average reference.

  6. Epochs run from −200 to 600 ms with baseline −200 to 0 ms. An epoch is rejected if any channel exceeds ±100 µV after baseline correction.

  7. A cleaned continuous copy (125 Hz) is also saved for spectral, connectivity and complexity features. It is cut into 2-s segments, and segments with any |x| > 100 µV are dropped.

Participant exclusion criteria (fixed now, applied mechanically)#

A participant is excluded if any of these holds:

  • (a) more than 20% of channels are bad (more than 25 of 129);

  • (b) fewer than 50% of trials remain (fewer than 240 of 480);

  • (c) fewer than 40 trials remain in any condition.

Excluded participants are reported, and nothing else is changed afterwards.

Features (subject level, computed per participant without using labels)#

  • Regions: 10 regions (L/R × frontal, central, temporal, parietal, occipital), defined from montage coordinates. Midline electrodes are assigned to neither hemisphere.

  • Spectral: Welch power on 2-s segments for delta 1–4, theta 4–8, alpha 8–13, beta 13–30 and low gamma 30–40 Hz. Both log absolute and relative power are computed, per region.

  • Frontal alpha asymmetry: ln α(E124 ≈ F4) − ln α(E24 ≈ F3), plus the F8/F7 pair (E122/E33).

  • ERP per condition, on per-condition averages:

    • N170: minimum at E58/E96 (≈P7/P8), 140–200 ms; amplitude and latency.

    • P300: mean amplitude 250–500 ms and peak latency at a centro-parietal cluster (Pz ≈ E62 plus neighbours).

    • LPP: mean amplitude 400–600 ms, same cluster.

  • Connectivity: wPLI between the 10 region-mean signals in the theta, alpha and beta bands.

  • Complexity: per region, mean over segments of sample entropy, Higuchi fractal dimension and Lempel-Ziv complexity.

Evaluation (all subject-wise)#

  • Outer loop: StratifiedGroupKFold, 5 folds, repeated 10 times (seeds 0–9). Each fold asserts that train and test participants are disjoint.

  • Inner loop: hyperparameters are tuned with 5-fold subject-grouped CV inside the training participants. Scaling, imputation and feature selection all live inside a sklearn Pipeline.

  • Trial-level models average their trial probabilities to give one probability per participant.

  • Metrics (per participant): balanced accuracy at a fixed 0.5 threshold, ROC AUC, sensitivity, specificity, F1 and Brier score.

    • Each metric is computed per repetition and averaged.

    • 95% CI from 2,000 participant-level bootstrap resamples of the out-of-fold predictions, averaged over repetitions.

    • p-value from a label-permutation test: subject labels permuted 1,000 times with the full nested pipeline re-run, using 1 repetition for speed. Deep models get fewer permutations, and the number is reported.

  • Primary model, fixed a priori: L2-regularised logistic regression on the full subject-level handcrafted feature set.

  • Primary outcome: its subject-level ROC AUC. Every other model is secondary.

    • The model with the best mean AUC is reported as such.

    • Because picking the best model inflates its score, a fully nested “select the model family in the inner loop” estimate is also reported.

  • Secondary models:

    • Demographic baseline: age, sex and education, logistic regression.

    • L1-LR, linear SVM, RBF SVM, Random Forest and LightGBM (max depth 3).

    • Riemannian: trial covariances of region signals → tangent space + LR, and MDM.

    • Deep learning (braindecode): EEGNet, ShallowFBCSPNet and TCN. These use early stopping on an inner subject-wise validation split.

  • Invalid control: the best trial-level model re-evaluated with a trial-wise split, only to measure inflation. It is always labelled invalid.

Confounds#

  • Education differs between groups (p = 0.005), so it is part of the demographic baseline.

  • Recording period is partly confounded with group (Oct 2015–Jan 2016: only HC; Mar–Apr 2016: only MDD). Two controls:

    • Can EEG features predict the recording period (before vs after Sep 2015)?

    • A sensitivity analysis restricted to participants recorded June–August 2015.

  • Further checks:

    • Can EEG features predict sex and age (median split)?

    • Performance broken down by sex.

    • Group differences in data quality: bad channels, ICA components removed and trials retained.

  • Questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7, CTQ-SF, LES, SSRS, PSQI) are never used as predictors.

Interpretation#

  • Permutation importance computed inside the CV test folds, and SHAP on the primary model.

  • Channel-level group effect sizes are shown as topomaps.

  • Grand-average ERPs by group and condition, with a spatio-temporal cluster permutation test. This is descriptive only.
